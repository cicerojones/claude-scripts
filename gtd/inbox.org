#+TITLE: Inbox
#+STARTUP: overview

Capture inbox. See ../gtd/CONVENTIONS.org for the schema.

Automated captures (via capture.py) are appended below this line as
flat top-level headlines. Nothing above this point is ever edited by
automation.

Example of what capture.py appends (shown as a comment block so it
never appears as a real task in your agenda):

#+begin_example
* TODO Call dentist about appointment                          :@calls:
:PROPERTIES:
:CREATED:  [2026-08-20 Thu 10:15]
:CAPTURED-BY: claude
:END:
#+end_example
